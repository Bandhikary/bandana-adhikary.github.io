Mentoring

Growing Tales

Manager and mentor at Growing Tales, a learning centre for children from eight months to eighteen years, where I run the studio and teach dance, theatre and NextGen classes.

Role
Manager and mentor
Organisation
Growing Tales
Location
Kathmandu

Growing Tales is a holistic learning centre in Kathmandu for children from eight months to eighteen years old. I have been there six months, as manager and as mentor. The two jobs pull in opposite directions and are better held together.

The managing is the studio schedule, the programme calendar, the workshop plans, and everything the audience sees: reels, posts, posters. Making sure things happen when they said they would.

The mentoring is slower, and it is the part I came for. I teach dance, theatre and our NextGen classes. I have run mother-and-toddler open houses, and staged two theatre productions with the children, one for Krishna Janmashtami and one for Halloween. A good deal of the work involves being eight years old myself for an hour at a time.

Mentoring is the clearest version of what I believe about education. You cannot deliver it. You can only stay in the room, pay attention, and ask the question the child is already circling.
