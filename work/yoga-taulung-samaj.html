Yoga

Yoga at Taulung Samaj

I teach yoga at Taulung Samaj every Wednesday, to a community class of fifty and more.

Role
Yoga teacher
Organisation
Taulung Samaj
Tradition
Patanjali

Every Wednesday I teach a community yoga class at Taulung Samaj. More than fifty people come.

I trained in the Patanjali tradition, fifteen days of intensive training in Kathmandu and ten more in Haridwar. I teach it as a practice rather than as exercise: breath first, attention second, and the posture last, as the thing that makes the first two possible.

Teaching a class of fifty has taught me something a university classroom could not. The people in that room owe me nothing and are not being assessed. They come back because the practice gives them something, which is, I think, the standard every classroom should be held to.
