Sound

Sound healing practice

Singing bowls, tone and silence. Sessions for individuals, for schools through TEN, and above all for teachers.

Practice
Himalayan singing bowls
Trained under
Santosh Guru
Location
Kathmandu

I work with sound: Himalayan singing bowls, tone, and the silence around them. I trained under Santosh Guru, I practise daily on my own, and I hold sessions for people who ask for them.

The plain description is that sustained tone slows breathing, and slowed breathing slows thought. People arrive carrying a week and leave able to hear themselves again.

I came to this through education, not away from it. A classroom in Kathmandu is a loud place, and most of what we ask of children, attention, patience, the ability to sit with something difficult, we never actually teach them. Through TEN I have run sound sessions in several schools. Sound is one of the few tools I have found that teaches attention directly, in the body, without a word of instruction.

I work most often with teachers. I know how drained a teacher can be by the end of a term, and how little of anyone's attention goes to the person standing at the front of the room.
